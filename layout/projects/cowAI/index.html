What is BovAi?

In recent years, AI technology has improved by leaps and bounds but with all this progress, there has been one clear shortcoming: all AIs have been trained exclusively on human conversation.

until now!

BovAi has been trained on dozens of seconds of cattle conversation. The technolgy of yesteryear would require you to drive at least a mile just to talk with a cow but now you can have an identical experience from the comfort of your suburban home!
